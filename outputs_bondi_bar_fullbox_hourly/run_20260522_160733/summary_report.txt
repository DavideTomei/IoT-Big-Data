Big Data context:
The application converts raw video frames into compact structured time-series rows and event records. In a larger IoT pipeline, many camera nodes could publish the same schema through MQTT or Kafka. The data could then be stored in a data lake, processed by Spark/Flink, and visualized in dashboards for tourism activity monitoring, venue capacity analysis, footfall-to-customer conversion, and local business planning.

Privacy note:
The system stores aggregate counts and events, not face identities or raw personal images. This is more privacy-preserving than storing continuous video.
